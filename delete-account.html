Delete your My Mentor account

App: My Mentor (com.arpanbohra.mymentor), by Arpan Bohra & Co.

Fastest: inside the app

  1. Open My Mentor and go to the Me tab.
  2. Scroll to Privacy and tap Delete all my data.
  3. Confirm. Your account and everything stored for it are permanently deleted from our servers and from the phone.

If you cannot open the app

Email office.abandco.ai@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my My Mentor account". We will delete the account and all associated data and confirm by email, normally within 7 days.

What is deleted

Your sign-in, profile, habits, check-ins, goals, planner, practice history, saved memories, reports data, and anything you shared in Couple Space. If you were linked with a partner, the link ends and your shared items disappear for both of you. Nothing is kept for advertising or analytics.

If you never signed in, nothing is stored on our servers; uninstalling the app removes the data on your device.

## Full description (max 4000)
My Mentor is your private accountability partner. It helps you plan your day, build habits, practise difficult conversations and grow your work, all in English, Hindi or Hinglish.

WHAT YOU GET
• Hourly check-ins and a daily One Thing: record what you actually did, honestly. Missing days are never counted as failures; unrecorded is shown as unrecorded.
• Habits with Daily, Weekly, Monthly and Yearly views, streaks with a forgiving shield, colour coding, minimum "bad day" versions and a focus timer.
• Goals from year to week, with progress that rolls up, and a daily planner with reminders before timed items.
• Krishna, your mentor: talk or type. Krishna uses only what you have recorded, pushes you firmly but respectfully on slipping habits and goals, and never shames you. Works with AI when you turn it on, and from your own records when you do not.
• Communication Lab: 24 real situations (client conversations, seminars, professional English, assertive family talks). Speak or type, get a score on five fixed criteria, and try again.
• Work and Business: log growth actions, track where your time goes, get delegation advice from your own numbers, and keep a due-date calendar for common compliance deadlines (always verify dates on the official portal).
• Couple Space: daily small acts and quotes in three languages, conversation guides, rituals, repair phrases, and an optional private shared space where both of you must agree before anything is shared. No scores. No comparisons.
• Reports: weekly, monthly and 30-day reviews with a plain-language explanation of every number, PDF export, XP, levels and badges.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account is needed. Without one, everything stays on your phone.
• AI is off until you switch it on. Names, PAN, GSTIN, phone numbers and similar details are masked before anything is sent.
• No ads, no trackers, no selling of data. Delete your account and data any time inside the app.

NOT A SUBSTITUTE FOR PROFESSIONAL HELP
My Mentor offers coaching and reflection, not medical, legal or tax advice. If you are in crisis in India, call 112 or Tele-MANAS 14416.

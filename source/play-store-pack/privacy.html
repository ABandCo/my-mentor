My Mentor: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026. Operated by Arpan Bohra & Co., Chartered Accountants, India. Contact: office.abandco.ai@gmail.com

My Mentor is a private accountability, habits and communication-practice app. This page explains what information the app uses, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.

1. You can use the app without an account

If you do not sign in, everything you enter (habits, goals, plans, notes, practice answers) stays only on your own device. Nothing is sent to our servers.

Your data is stored in a database hosted by Supabase in Mumbai, India. Only you can read your own rows; this is enforced by the database itself (row-level security).

3. AI features

If you switch on the AI mentor, the app sends a short, masked summary of your recorded habits, plans and the text you type to Google's Gemini service through our server, so it can reply. Before anything is sent, the app replaces PAN, GSTIN, IFSC, email addresses, phone numbers, Aadhaar-style numbers, bank account numbers, and any client or family names you list in Settings. Health, family and journal categories are sent only if you separately allow it. If a message suggests self-harm, the app does not send it to the AI and shows emergency help numbers instead. With Google's free Gemini tier, Google may use submitted content to improve its products, so please do not type information you do not want shared. AI is off by default and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.

4. Microphone

If you tap and hold the microphone button, your phone's own speech recogniser converts your voice to text. My Mentor does not record or store audio. Only the recognised text is used, in the same way as text you type. The recogniser may be provided by Google on your device and is governed by your device settings.

5. Notifications

Reminders are created on your phone. We do not use push-notification servers or advertising identifiers.

6. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the app: Supabase (database and sign-in) and Google (Gemini AI, only when you enable it). We do not sell or rent personal data. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

You can export your data and delete your account at any time inside the app (Me, then "Delete all my data"). This permanently deletes your account and all your data on our servers and on your device. You can also request deletion without the app: see how to delete your account. Deletion removes your rows from the live database.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Each account's data is isolated by database rules, and the AI key never reaches your phone. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password.

9. Children

My Mentor is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Not professional advice

The app offers coaching and reflection, not medical, legal, tax or mental-health advice. If you are in crisis, call 112 or Tele-MANAS 14416 in India.

11. Changes and contact

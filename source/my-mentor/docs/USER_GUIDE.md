# User guide (draft)

**Home** — Write today's One Thing. Each hour, write what you'll do, then tap Done / Partly / Missed. Tap a habit tile to mark it done. Tap the emoji row to log your energy.

**Habits** — Add from templates or create your own. Each day choose Done, Partial, Missed, Rest day or N/A. Days you don't record are shown as "unrecorded", never as failures.

**Streaks** — One missed day per week is protected by a shield. Coming back after a gap counts as a Comeback, which matters more than a perfect streak.

**Privacy** — Right now everything stays on your phone.

This guide grows with each phase.


## What is in My Mentor 1.2.0
- **Home**: One Thing, this hour, habits, Krishna's push of the day, shortcuts.
- **Habits**: Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Yearly views, tap circles to log, press and hold for partial/missed/rest.
- **Goals and Planner** (icons on Habits): year-to-week goals, timed plan items with reminders.
- **Mentor** (centre orb): type or hold the mic to talk. Works with AI after you sign in and add the Gemini key.
- **Grow**: Communication Lab, Goals, Planner, Work & Business, Couple Space, Reports, Memory.
- **Me / Settings**: language, coaching style, privacy, reminders, export, delete.
- You do not need an account. Sign in only if you want the AI mentor, backup, or Couple Space.
